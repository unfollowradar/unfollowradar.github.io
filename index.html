1. Get your data from Instagram (free, official)

Instagram doesn't let any website pull your lists from just a username, and scraping breaks its rules and can get accounts locked. Instagram's own data download has the full lists, so this page uses that.

  1. Instagram app: Settings > Accounts Center > Your information and permissions > Download your information.
  2. Choose Some of your information, tick Followers and following, set format to JSON, date range All time.
  3. When Instagram emails the download (minutes to a day), unzip it and open the connections/followers_and_following folder.
